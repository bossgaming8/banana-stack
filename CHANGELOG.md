# Changelog

## 1.0.0 (Minecraft 26.3)

The first release.

- Walk up behind a player and right-click them with an empty hand to ride on their head.
- Right-click the bottom of a stack to climb to the top. Stacks can be as tall as the server allows.
- Press Shift to hop off; riders land on the ground beside the stack. At the bottom, Shift shakes everyone off.
- When someone leaves the middle of a stack, the players above slide down instead of falling.
- Riders sit just above the carrier's eyes, so the carrier can still mine, build and fight. Under low ceilings they duck down, and bump off when there's no room.
- Teleports, portals, logging out, dying, spectator mode and sleeping all leave the stack cleanly, and riders are never carried off with a teleporting player.
- `/stack on`, `/stack off` and `/stack toggle` to choose whether others can ride you, and `/stack reload` for operators.
- LuckPerms-friendly permission nodes, a small config file, and support for the Vanish mod.
- Server-side: players connect with a normal vanilla client.
